# 📈 Forever Enhanced XP Bar

An XP bar for World of Warcraft: Forever that shows what's coming as well as what you have: the XP waiting in your finished quests, how far your rested XP reaches, your XP per hour and how long until your next level.

## ✦ What it does

- 🟪 **The bar:** your XP in a blue-violet to violet gradient, then the XP waiting in finished quests in amber, then your rested XP in blue, on a dark rounded track. Each part stops at the end of the bar. Amber stripes are an option.
- 🔢 **On the bar:** your level, your XP, and your % with, in brackets, your % once your finished quests are handed in.
- 📊 **Under the bar:** your completed quests and your rested XP, each as a share of the level, in their bar colours. On a narrow bar it uses shorter words, so it always fits.
- 🔎 **Hover it** for the details: XP this level, rested XP, XP in finished quests, quests done, time on this level, this session, XP per hour, next level in, kills to level and quests to level. Tick only the ones you want.
- ⏱️ **Time on this level** is counted while you play. It's exact once you level up with the addon on, or after you type `/played` once.
- 📏 **Your size and place:** Thin, Normal, Thick or Large, any width, and drag it anywhere. On first use it sits just above your bottom action bars (or above the cast bar with EraUI's classic bar, when there isn't room under it).
- 🔤 **Text on the bar:** always, on hover or never; numbers, percent or both. A Thin bar has its text just above it. On a narrow bar your XP numbers make way first, so your level and % stay.
- 🏆 **At max level** it can stay as a gold bar that still shows the XP waiting in finished quests.
- 🙈 **Hide the game's own XP bar** (experimental): fades it out and puts it back the moment you untick it.

## 💬 Commands

- `/xpbar` opens the settings (`/fexb` works too)
- `/xpbar move` lets you drag the bar
- `/xpbar reset` puts the bar back above your action bars
- `/xpbar minimap` shows or hides the minimap button
- `/xpbar session` starts a new session (type it twice)

## ℹ️ Good to know

- Rested XP only boosts XP from monsters, so the blue part comes after the quest part.
- XP per hour uses the last hour by default; the settings can change it to the last 15 minutes or the whole session.
- Kills to level is worked out from your last kills, so it settles after a few.
- Numbers are kept for each character; settings are shared by all of them.
- With EraUI's classic bar on, the game's XP bar is part of its stone band, so it stays.
