# Changelog

## 1.0.0

### Added
- An XP bar with the XP waiting in finished quests and your rested XP on it.
- Your level, XP and % on the bar, plus your % once your finished quests are handed in.
- A line under the bar with your completed quests and rested XP as a share of the level.
- Hover the bar for XP this level, rested, quests done, time on this level, this session, XP per hour and when you'll level.
- Kills and quests to level, from your last kills and hand-ins.
- Thin, Normal, Thick or Large, any width, drag it anywhere; text always, on hover or never.
- The game's own XP bar fades out (on by default; untick it to bring it back).
- Optional: keep the bar at max level.
